# Race audio attribution

`william-tell-finale.mp3` is an excerpt (finale only, loudness-normalized) of:

- Work: Gioachino Rossini, William Tell Overture (military band
  transcription by Wenzel Sedlak). The composition is public domain.
- Recording: The United States Marine Band, conducted by Timothy Foley,
  album _Grand Scenes_ (2000). A work of the U.S. federal government:
  public domain in the United States.
- Source file: `File:Gioachino Rossini, William Tell Overture (military
  band version, 2000).ogg` on Wikimedia Commons
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gioachino_Rossini,_William_Tell_Overture_(military_band_version,_2000).ogg).

The `horse-*.png` images were generated for this project with an image model.
